FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about our services and care.

What services do you offer?
We offer a comprehensive range of dental services including preventive care, restorative dentistry, endodontics, prosthodontics, dental implants, periodontics, oral surgery, orthodontics, pediatric dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and 24/7 emergency care.
Do you accept insurance?
Yes, we work with most major insurance providers. Please contact our front desk for specific details about your insurance coverage and we'll be happy to assist you.
How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by clicking the "Book Now" button in the navigation, calling us directly, or visiting our clinic in person. We also offer instant booking via our Telegram bot @ToriDentalBot.
Do you treat children?
Absolutely! We have a dedicated pediatric dentistry service with a child-friendly environment and gentle approach to make dental visits a positive experience for children of all ages.
